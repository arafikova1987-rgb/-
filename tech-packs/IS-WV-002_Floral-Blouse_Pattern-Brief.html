1 · Brief

Develop a production-ready base pattern in size XS–S (RU 42–44) for a semi-relaxed short-sleeve blouse in printed woven fabric. Cut-on (grown-on) short sleeves, 2 cm stand collar with a single ruffle, front inset bib with self-fabric piping and a ruffled 7-button placket. Straight hem, 60 cm at centre back. Overlock construction.

Deliverables:

  • PDF pattern 1:1, A4 tiled with registration marks and A0 sheet; both with seam allowances included and marked; piece name, size, grain, qty, notches, fold lines on every piece.
  • Pattern piece list with cut quantities and fusing plan (table 6).
  • Pattern measurements checked against table 4; report any point the pattern cannot meet.
  • Optional: DXF (AAMA) export for grading later.

Fit target: bust 84–88, waist 66–70, hip 92–96, height 164–172. Ease at chest approx. +16–20 cm, straight body, no darts, no waist shaping. Pull-over with placket buttoned open is not required: garment opens fully at placket to bib bottom; neck opening with placket unbuttoned must pass over head (≥ 60 cm opening circumference).

5 · Construction notes for the pattern

AreaInstruction
Front bodyCut ×2 (mirror). CF opening from collar seam to bib bottom (27 cm placket). Bib shape removed from the front as an inset (recommended) so the bib seam carries piping and lies flat; alternative appliqué bib only if the fabric proves too light for an inset seam – pattern maker to advise. Below the bib the two fronts are joined at CF (seam or CF on fold below the bib – pattern maker's choice, mark on pattern).
Back bodyCut ×1 on fold at CB. No darts, no yoke, no CB seam.
Cut-on sleeveShoulder line extended 15 cm beyond shoulder point, sleeve angle approx. 20° below horizontal so the sleeve hangs without pulling; slight over-sleeve shaping allowed. Sleeve hem straight, 18 cm half opening. Underarm curve smooth into side seam at 25 cm below HPS; add 1 cm gusset-free ease in underarm curve for movement.
Bib panelCut ×2 (mirror), pointed oval, 30 cm at CF, 22 cm widest at 10–12 cm below collar seam, inner edge = placket seam line. Grain: straight grain parallel to CF.
PlacketTwo-piece placket, each 2.5 cm finished, folded band (cut 2× width + SA), both outer edges piped. Wearer's right = buttonhole side, over wearer's left = button side, full overlap 2.5 cm. Bottom of placket closed and enclosed inside the bib seam / point.
RufflesAll single-layer, straight grain, gathering ratio 1:2, finished widths: collar 2.5, bib/placket 3.5, sleeve 5. Add 0.7 cm SA on attachment edge and 0.6 cm for narrow rolled hem on free edge. Bib ruffle inserted into placket seam (between placket and bib), running full placket length and meeting at the bib point.
CollarStand collar 2 cm finished, front ends straight (no button on stand), CB on fold. Cut outer + inner stand; ruffle and piping in top edge seam. Neck edge length = neckline of body (approx. 40 cm) + 2 × placket width at CF.
PipingFlat self-fabric piping, 0.4 cm finished, no cord; bias strip 2.2 cm. Applied in: bib inset seam, both placket edges, collar top edge.
HemStraight, 1 cm finished double fold (cut 2 cm allowance).

7 · Fabric information for the pattern maker

Printed viscose / cotton-viscose jacquard, approx. 130–150 g/m², width 140–145 cm, non-stretch, brand supplied. Directional floral print: mark pattern pieces "one-way" and note print matching is not required, but avoid cutting a large flower head on the bib centre. Pre-wash shrinkage to be tested by brand and reported; pattern to include shrinkage allowance if > 2%. TBC Final fabric to be confirmed before grading.
