1 · Style description

Semi-relaxed short-sleeve blouse in printed woven fabric. Cut-on (grown-on) sleeves finished with a 5 cm self ruffle. 2 cm stand collar with a single 2.5 cm ruffle and self piping. Front inset bib outlined with self-fabric flat piping; 2.5 cm piped button placket with a 3.5 cm gathered ruffle on each side; 7 gold shank buttons. Straight hem 60 cm at CB. Overlock construction.

  • Fabric: supplied by brand (printed viscose / cotton-viscose jacquard). Factory supplies interlining, thread, packing materials.
  • Pattern: supplied by brand in PDF 1:1 – print on A0 or tile A4, verify 10 cm test square before cutting.
  • Buttons: supplied by brand.
  • Sample: 1 pc XS–S in bulk fabric (brand ships 2.5 m) with all trims.

1Cut per marker, one-way. Fuse collar outer stand and both placket bands. Cut bias strips 2.2 cm, join on straight grain, press.Fusing press, viscose-safe temperature (test on scrap first)
2Prepare flat piping: fold bias strip in half, press; finished 0.4 cm visible. Approx. 2.3 m per piece.—
3Prepare ruffles (collar ×1, bib ×2, sleeve ×2): narrow rolled hem on free edge; two rows of gathering stitch at 0.5 / 0.9 cm; gather to 1:2 evenly, no pleating.3-thread rolled hem overlock; lockstitch 4 spi gathering
4Placket bands: attach piping to both long edges of each band; fold band, press. Attach bib ruffle to inner edge of each band (ruffle between band and bib), then join band + ruffle unit to bib panel. Press seam toward bib; edge-stitch 0.1 on bib side to hold ruffle down.Lockstitch 12 spi, SA 1.0 / 0.7, zipper foot for piping
5Attach piping to outer edge of assembled bib. Set bib into front cut-out (inset seam), matching notches; clip curves. Overlock seam allowance together, press toward body, stitch-in-ditch beside piping to keep flat. Close CF below bib.Lockstitch SA 1.0; 3-thread overlock
6Shoulder / over-sleeve seams: front to back, one continuous seam from neck to sleeve hem. Overlock, press to back.4-thread overlock / lockstitch + overlock, SA 1.0
7Sleeve ruffles: join ruffle ends, attach to sleeve hem right sides together, gather even; overlock SA, press toward body, edge-stitch 0.1 on body.Lockstitch SA 0.7 + 3-thread overlock
8Side / underarm seams in one pass, matching sleeve ruffle seam and hem. Insert care label at wearer's left side seam 10 cm above hem. Overlock, press to back.4-thread overlock, SA 1.0
9Collar: piping + collar ruffle sandwiched between outer (fused) and inner stand along top edge; stitch, trim, turn, press. Attach outer stand to neckline with main label at CB; turn inner stand in, edge-stitch 0.1 all round.Lockstitch 12 spi, SA 1.0 / 0.7
10Hem: 1 cm double-fold, edge-stitch 0.1, seam allowances turned toward back.Lockstitch 12 spi
11Buttonholes ×7 on wearer's right placket, vertical, 1.7 cm, centred, first 1.5 cm below collar seam, spacing 3.8 cm. Buttons ×7 on left placket, shank sewn with thread shank wrap. Spare button inside at care label.Buttonhole machine, button-sew

8 · Quality requirements & QC points

  • Measurements within tolerance (section 5). Both fronts identical: bib and ruffle mirror symmetrically; check at CF – max. difference 0.3 cm.
  • Ruffles evenly gathered, no flat or bunched sections; gathers steamed, not pressed flat.
  • Piping uniform 0.4 cm, no wobble; corners at bib point neat and mitred.
  • Collar stand sits flat, ends level at CF, no twist.
  • Buttonholes clean, matched to buttons, no gaping between buttons when worn.
  • Print: no visible flaws, no large flower head cut through at bib centre; one-way direction on all pieces.
  • Seams: no puckering, no pulled threads (needle 70/10), all overlock ends secured.
  • Pressing: no shine, no crushed ruffles; garment free of odour, threads, marks; metal detection passed.
  • Inspection AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor; size set and 3 pcs from bulk photographed before shipment.

Sample & approval process

  1. Quotation within 3 working days of receiving this pack: unit price FOB at 300 / 500 pcs per colour, MOQ per colour, sample cost and lead time.
  2. Pre-production sample (PPS / fit sample) 1 pc in sample size in available materials closest to reference. Send photos front / back / detail on dress form and flat before shipping. Sample ships with measurement chart filled in by factory QC.
  3. Brand review: measurements against tolerance table, construction, hand-feel. Comments returned within 5 working days. Second sample only if fit changes are required.
  4. Colour approval: lab-dips / yarn shade card swatches for 2 colourways approved before bulk material is ordered.
  5. Bulk: production only after written sample + colour approval. Size set (1 pc per size) photographed before bulk sewing. Inline and final inspection AQL 2.5.
  6. Deviations: any change of material, construction or measurement must be confirmed in writing by the brand before production.
