1 · Style description

Cropped, semi-relaxed fit fully-fashioned cardigan with drop shoulders. Knitted point collar and 4 cm button placket in a two-colour jacquard diamond check. All-over intarsia tulip motif on both fronts, back and sleeves. 1x1 rib hem and cuffs with two white stripes. 5 buttons.

  • Fit: semi-relaxed / slightly oversized, cropped to natural waist, drop shoulder, straight body.
  • Construction: fully fashioned panels, linking on all seams (no cut-and-sew). Placket and collar knitted separately and linked on.
  • Motif: tulips must be knitted in (intarsia), clean back with no long floats. Embroidery, appliqué or print are NOT accepted.
  • Check placket/collar: jacquard (2-colour + white) – float jacquard or ladder-back acceptable if reverse is neat; factory to reproduce the check from the reference photo and submit a swatch.
  • Reference: photo attached (page 2). Reproduce silhouette, proportions and motif placement 1:1.

9 · Quality requirements

  • Measurements within tolerance (table 5); all 5 sizes of a size set measured and recorded.
  • Intarsia: no holes at colour changes, no floats longer than 1 cm on the reverse, ends secured and trimmed.
  • Rib: 1x1, even tension, must recover after 20% stretch; white stripes straight and continuous across seams.
  • Linking: no skipped stitches; shoulder and neck seams stabilised (tape or chain) to prevent stretching.
  • Collar and placket lie flat, no twisting; buttonholes aligned to buttons, no gaping at chest.
  • Hand-feel: soft, not scratchy; pilling test ≥ 3–4 (ISO 12945-2, 2000 rubs); no shedding.
  • Colour fastness to washing ≥ 4, no migration of pink into white petals.
  • Steamed, folded, no odour, no loose threads, metal-detector passed.

Sample & approval process

  1. Quotation within 3 working days of receiving this pack: unit price FOB at 300 / 500 pcs per colour, MOQ per colour, sample cost and lead time.
  2. Pre-production sample (PPS / fit sample) 1 pc in sample size in available materials closest to reference. Send photos front / back / detail on dress form and flat before shipping. Sample ships with measurement chart filled in by factory QC.
  3. Brand review: measurements against tolerance table, construction, hand-feel. Comments returned within 5 working days. Second sample only if fit changes are required.
  4. Colour approval: lab-dips / yarn shade card swatches for 2 colourways approved before bulk material is ordered.
  5. Bulk: production only after written sample + colour approval. Size set (1 pc per size) photographed before bulk sewing. Inline and final inspection AQL 2.5.
  6. Deviations: any change of material, construction or measurement must be confirmed in writing by the brand before production.
